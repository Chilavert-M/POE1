OUR STORY

The idea of entering the wine industry developed gradually. The three founders became fascinated by the relationship between the vineyard, the winemaker, and the final bottle. They discovered that wine was more than a drink—it was a combination of land, climate, craftsmanship, patience, and culture. They wanted Central Wines to represent exactly that. Each founder brought something different to the vision. Kluivert contributed leadership and a strong business mindset. Chilavert brought creativity and a passion for the wine itself, while Thomas brought determination, organisation, and the ability to turn ideas into action. Together, they began developing the concept of Central Wines.

Building Central Wines

Turning an idea into a business was not easy. The founders faced challenges along the way, from understanding the wine industry and developing their brand to establishing relationships and determining what they wanted their wines to represent. Instead of allowing these challenges to stop them, the three friends used them as opportunities to learn and grow. They wanted every part of Central Wines to have meaning. From the name and branding to the selection of wines, they focused on creating an identity that represented quality, authenticity, friendship, and ambition. Their Zimbabwean roots remained an important part of their story. They carried with them the values they had grown up with—community, resilience, respect, and the importance of sharing good moments with others.

Looking to the future

Today, the journey of Central Wines continues. What started as an idea shared between three Zimbabwean friends has grown into a brand with ambitions to reach wine lovers beyond its beginnings. Chilavert, Kluivert and Thomas continue to believe that the best businesses are built on more than products. They are built on relationships, purpose, passion, and a willingness to keep moving forward. Central Wines is therefore not simply about wine. It is about the people behind it, the experiences created around it, and the story that every bottle carries.
